About Me

I’m John, a lifelong car enthusiast with a questionable ability to justify buying another car. I’ve always loved cars for their design, performance, and the simple joy of making unnecessarily expensive noises. Over the years, that passion has turned into a business, where I get to work with cars I genuinely love so technically, I’ve made my hobby everyone else’s problem too.
